02 / ASSURANCE KERNEL

Hard rules stay hard.
The instructor stays in charge.

The agent does the production and brings back only the questions that need a person. Rights, accountable authorship, the approved plan, and the record of every decision are not negotiable.

  1. 01

    The plan is the instructor’s

    Each chapter’s concept map and assessment guide are approved in the instructor’s own words before the chapter is written. An approval knows what it approved; a later change comes back as a reviewable diff.

  2. 02

    Every sentence has an origin

    The package is indexed into units, each with an id, an author, a link to what it teaches and the kind of check it needs. An edit nobody recorded is raised, never overwritten.

  3. 03

    Facts checked blind, until quiet

    A fresh, context-blind pass of your own AI engine reads each passage as a student would. Fixes are rechecked until a pass raises nothing; arithmetic is recomputed and reactions balanced mechanically.

  4. 04

    Ideas, never expression

    Reference textbooks and prior notes are read for their ideas. No run of eight words in common with any reference ships; citations and equations are facts, not copying.

  5. 05

    A log that cannot be rewritten

    Approvals, answers, review dispatches and publish decisions go into a hash-chained log inside the package. Publication needs rights evidence, an accountable author and the instructor’s word.

HONEST BOUNDARY

Coursewerk finds the question.
It does not invent the answer.

A publisher’s AI-use notice is not automatically a copyright-license term. The pipeline preserves it with legal effect undetermined. Private work remains open; public preparation verifies the source facts and gives the instructor the warning and final decision.

05 / CLAIMS WITH EDGES

Automation is useful.
Overclaiming is not.

AUTO

Coursewerk can verify

  • Coverage of the approved plan, in both directions
  • Facts by a blind pass, rechecked until quiet
  • Arithmetic chains and balanced reactions
  • Copying from references, notation, syntax and links
  • Rights evidence, provenance, attribution and the decision log
HUMAN

Educators still decide

  • The plan: what to teach, in what order, at what level
  • Pedagogical fit for actual learners
  • Questions a blind check cannot settle
  • Legal interpretation and permissions
  • When, and whether, to publish
